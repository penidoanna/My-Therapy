How My Therapy works

1. Create your account

Patients and psychologists sign up with essential details. Professionals can complete their profiles and specialties.

2. Set availability & book

Psychologists define availability, patients pick their preferred times and receive confirmations and reminders.

3. Support with resources

Assign and access therapeutic resources such as videos, articles and exercises tailored to each patient.
